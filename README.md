# The 5am Diary toolbox

Free, practical tools for compliance, risk and financial-crime teams, from **The 5am Diary**: a founder building AI for compliance, one entry at a time.

Most writing about AI in compliance is theory. This is the other half: things I have built or use, shared so your team can use them the same day. You don't need to code. Each tool is a checklist, a prompt or a set of instructions you can paste into ChatGPT, Claude or a spreadsheet.

Every tool starts from a real problem compliance teams told me about. No client is named, and every example uses made-up data.

## How to use a tool

1. Open its folder and read `README.md`. It says what the tool does, what it can't do, and how long it takes.
2. Copy the checklist or the prompt.
3. Try it on made-up or already-closed cases first, never on live customer data in a tool your firm hasn't approved.

## The rules these tools follow

- **A person decides.** Every tool helps someone gather evidence or check their work. None makes the decision.
- **Honest limits.** Each tool says what it can't do.
- **Free to use and share.** Content is under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE): use it, change it, share it, and say where it came from.

## Not legal advice

These tools are shared to help teams fight financial crime. They are not legal or regulatory advice, and they don't replace your own policies, your regulator's guidance or your MLRO's judgement.
